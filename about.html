🌿 Our Speciality

🌿 Only Shadu Mati Ganpati Murti

At S.B. Joshi Enterprises, we provide only Shadu Mati Ganpati Murtis. We believe that devotion to Bappa and care for nature should go together. That is why we choose good-quality natural Shadu Mati for our Ganpati Murtis.

Shadu Mati is a natural clay material. It is a traditional material used for making Ganpati Murtis and is chosen by us as a more nature-conscious option for Ganeshotsav.

One of the special benefits of a Shadu Mati Murti is that you can perform home immersion in a suitable container and allow the clay to settle and dissolve naturally.

After the immersion, the clay can be collected and mixed appropriately with soil for your plants or trees. In this way, the clay can return to the soil instead of becoming unnecessary waste.
