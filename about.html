Our Ganpati Idols

🌿 Made Only from Shadu Mati

At S.B. Joshi Enterprises, we believe that celebrating Ganpati Bappa should be an experience filled with devotion, tradition, quality and care for our environment.

All our Ganpati idols are made using only Shadu Mati (natural clay). We choose Shadu Mati because it is a natural and eco-friendly material that is much better for our environment.

Shadu Mati idols can naturally dissolve in water after immersion and help reduce the environmental pollution caused by idols made from materials such as Plaster of Paris and other non-natural materials.

By choosing a Shadu Mati Ganpati idol, you are taking a small but meaningful step towards celebrating Ganesh Chaturthi in a more eco-friendly and responsible way.
