🌿 Our Speciality

Only Shadu Mati Ganpati Murtis

At S.B. Joshi Enterprises, we provide only Shadu Mati Ganpati Murtis. Shadu Mati is natural clay and is a traditional material used for making Ganpati Murtis.

We believe that devotion to Bappa and care for nature should go together. After suitable home immersion, the clay can be collected and appropriately mixed with soil for plants or trees.
