What type of Ganpati Murtis do you provide?

At S.B. Joshi Enterprises, we provide only Shadu Mati Ganpati Murtis. Our idols are made using good-quality natural Shadu Mati (natural clay), keeping tradition, quality and care for nature in mind.

Why do you provide Shadu Mati Murtis?

Shadu Mati is a traditional natural clay material. We choose Shadu Mati because we believe Ganpati Bappa celebration and care for nature can go together. A suitable home immersion can also allow the clay to settle and dissolve naturally.

Can I perform Shadu Mati Murti immersion at home?

Yes. A Shadu Mati Murti can be immersed at home in a suitable container. After the immersion, allow the clay to settle and dissolve naturally. The collected clay can then be appropriately mixed with soil and used around your plants or trees.
