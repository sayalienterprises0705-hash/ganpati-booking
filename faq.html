Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about booking and payment.

How can I book a Ganpati Murti?

Fill the booking form and click Submit Booking on WhatsApp. Your details will be prepared in a WhatsApp message to our business number.

Will the booking be automatically confirmed?

No. The first message is a booking request. It remains pending until our team reviews and confirms it manually.

Which payment modes are available?

The form provides Cash, GPay and PhonePe options.

Can I choose pickup or home delivery?

Yes. The booking form provides Pickup From Shop and Home Delivery options. Availability may depend on location.

Can I change my booking?

Contact us on 8856874068 as soon as possible with your booking ID.

Does the website store my booking?

No. This version has no database. The booking request is passed to WhatsApp. The selected payment screenshot is not transmitted by the website.
