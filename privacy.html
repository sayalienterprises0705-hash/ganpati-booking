Privacy Policy

Information about how this website handles customer information and booking requests.

1. Introduction

S.B. Joshi Enterprises respects the privacy of customers and visitors using this website.

This website is primarily designed to display Ganpati Bappa idols, Pooja Articles and provide a convenient way for customers to contact us and submit booking requests.

2. Information You Provide

When you use the booking form, you may enter information such as:

  • Customer name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Ganpati idol details
  • Size and quantity
  • Payment mode and advance amount
  • Preferred date
  • Address
  • Additional booking notes

3. How Booking Information Is Sent

The booking form is designed to prepare your booking information and open WhatsApp so that you can send the request directly to S.B. Joshi Enterprises.

The website itself does not require a Google Form, Google Sheets or a website database to submit the booking request.

4. WhatsApp

When you submit the booking form, WhatsApp may open with the booking details prepared in a message. You should review the message before sending it.

Information that you choose to send through WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

5. Payment Information

The website may display a UPI QR code and UPI ID for payment.

The website does not ask customers to enter their bank account password, UPI PIN or other confidential banking credentials into the website.

Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN or banking password with anyone.

6. Payment Screenshots

If you voluntarily send a payment screenshot to us through WhatsApp, that screenshot may contain transaction information. Please share only information necessary for payment verification.

7. Information Storage

This static website does not require a server-side customer database for the booking form.

Once you choose to send information through WhatsApp, the information may be retained in the relevant WhatsApp conversation or business records according to applicable practices.

8. Information Security

Please do not submit sensitive information such as passwords, UPI PINs, OTPs, bank passwords or card security codes through the booking form or WhatsApp.

9. Third-Party Services

This website may provide links or embedded content from services such as WhatsApp and Google Maps. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

10. Children's Information

The website is not specifically directed at collecting personal information from children. Parents or guardians should supervise children when providing personal information online.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when necessary. Any updated version will be published on this page.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or about information submitted through a booking request, please contact S.B. Joshi Enterprises.
